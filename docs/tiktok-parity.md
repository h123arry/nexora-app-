# Nexora short-form parity checklist

Nexora should feel as immediate and habit-forming as TikTok without copying TikTok's brand, assets, or visual identity. These are the 15 product capabilities to add or strengthen:

1. Full-screen vertical short-video feed with one clear item per viewport.
2. Separate **For You** and **Following** feeds with fast tab switching.
3. Visibility-aware autoplay that plays only the active item and pauses on navigation.
4. Swipe, wheel, keyboard, and Android-back navigation between videos.
5. Double-tap spark/like feedback with optimistic updates.
6. Persistent mute state plus tap-for-sound behavior.
7. Right-side action rail for sparks, comments, saves, shares, and more actions.
8. Creator identity overlay with avatar, verification, follow, and profile entry points.
9. Caption truncation, hashtags, sound attribution, and tappable discovery context.
10. Explainable recommendation ranking based on watch time, completion, rewatches, and interests.
11. Lightweight watch history and resume position for unfinished videos.
12. Share sheet that supports direct messages and native share where available.
13. Comment drawer with threaded replies and quick reactions.
14. Creator camera flow for recording, trimming, captioning, and publishing short videos.
15. Safety and control tools: not interested, mute creator, report, block, content quality filters, and accessible keyboard labels.

The current phase wires the explainable ranking foundation into the existing Reels path. Later phases should keep these behaviors composable with Firebase persistence, Nexora's recommendation events, and the existing creator tools.
