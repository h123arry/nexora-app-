# GiftVerse artwork rights and adaptation policy

GiftVerse is a commercial NEXORA feature. An image being publicly viewable or downloadable does not grant permission to redistribute, modify or sell it.

## Import requirements

Every imported artwork must have a provenance record containing:
- the exact source URL and source filename;
- the original creator or rights holder;
- the exact licence and licence URL;
- a plain-language description of the changes made;
- the final GiftVerse gift ID and bundled asset path.

Only verified licences that permit commercial use and adaptation are accepted by the import pipeline: CC0-1.0, CC BY 4.0, CC BY-SA 4.0, MIT, Apache-2.0 and public-domain material. A source is rejected if its rights cannot be verified. Non-commercial, no-derivatives, personal-use-only and unclear licences are not accepted.

## Attribution and share-alike

- CC BY 4.0: credit the creator, link the licence and identify material modifications.
- CC BY-SA 4.0: meet the attribution requirements and distribute the adapted artwork under the same or a compatible share-alike licence. Do not describe that adapted artwork as exclusively owned by NEXORA.
- Apache-2.0: preserve required copyright, licence and NOTICE material where supplied.
- MIT: preserve the applicable copyright and permission notice.
- CC0 and public-domain: retain source provenance internally even where attribution is not legally required.

The importer records provenance and blocks missing or unapproved licence metadata. It removes active SVG content and external resource references before bundling. It does not itself establish that a source licence is genuine; a human rights review is still required before import.

## NEXORA originality standard

A palette swap alone is not a unique gift. Adaptations should create a materially distinct composition, silhouette, material treatment or scene, with a new gift identity and original description. Do not reuse one source image for multiple catalogue entries. Do not import logos, branded characters, celebrity likenesses or artwork with unclear ownership.

## Current catalogue status

The existing 120 bundled catalogue images predate this import pipeline and are treated as existing NEXORA artwork. No third-party image is claimed as newly imported by this document. New imported batches must include their provenance records alongside the final assets.
