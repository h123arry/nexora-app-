# GiftVerse artwork rights and adaptation policy

GiftVerse is a commercial NEXORA feature. Public visibility or downloadability alone does not grant permission to redistribute, modify or sell an image.

## Twemoji source collection

The 880 imported Twemoji illustrations are pinned to a fixed upstream commit. Twemoji states that its graphics are licensed under CC BY 4.0 and requests attribution in a project README, About section or footer. GiftVerse records per-image source metadata in `provenance/twemoji-v15.1.0.json` and credits the project in [TWEMOJI_ATTRIBUTION.md](./TWEMOJI_ATTRIBUTION.md).

The original Twemoji SVGs are currently referenced at their pinned upstream URLs, not copied into the repository. NEXORA's distinct treatment is the catalogue identity, description, rarity, NEX price, midnight-violet presentation frame and animation composition. This does not transfer exclusive rights in the source illustrations to NEXORA. Remote hosting means these 880 artworks require network access.

## Import requirements

Every imported artwork must record its exact source URL and version, original creator or rights holder, exact licence and licence URL, a plain-language description of NEXORA's changes, and the final gift ID. Only verified licences that permit commercial use and adaptation are accepted. Non-commercial, no-derivatives, personal-use-only and unclear licences are rejected.

- CC BY 4.0: credit the creator, link the licence and identify modifications.
- CC BY-SA 4.0: meet attribution requirements and distribute adapted artwork under the same or a compatible share-alike licence.
- Apache-2.0 and MIT: preserve applicable copyright, licence and NOTICE material.
- CC0 and public-domain: retain source provenance internally even where attribution is not legally required.

The SVG importer strips active SVG content and external resource references before local bundling. It does not itself establish that a source licence is genuine; human rights review remains necessary.

## NEXORA originality standard

A palette swap alone is not a unique gift. Each GiftVerse entry must have its own source illustration or original composition, stable identity, image reference, description, category, rarity and NEX price. Do not import logos, branded characters, celebrity likenesses or artwork with unclear ownership.
